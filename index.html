THE VRAT, IN ORDER

Chhath in four days.

A simple, at-a-glance guide to the four-day observance. Family and regional customs may vary.

01
🌿

नहाय खाय

Nahay Khay

The home and kitchen are prepared, and the vrat begins with a simple, sattvic meal.

DAY ONE · PURIFICATION
02
🥣

खरना

Kharna

After a day of fasting, devotees traditionally prepare prasad such as kheer and roti at dusk.

DAY TWO · PRASAD
03
🌅

संध्या अर्घ्य

Sandhya Arghya

Offerings are made to the setting sun at the ghat, with family and community gathered by the water.

DAY THREE · SETTING SUN
04
☀️

उषा अर्घ्य

Usha Arghya

At sunrise, arghya is offered to the rising sun, followed by the traditional completion of the vrat.

PRASAD MADE AT HOME

Thekua, made for sharing.

A practical starting recipe for homemade thekua. Ingredient amounts are approximate; adjust flour and liquid gradually for the dough.

Simple method

  1. Dissolve jaggery in a little warm water and let it cool.
  2. Mix flour, coconut, fennel and ghee; add jaggery water gradually to make a firm dough.
  3. Shape small portions with a mould or fork pattern.
  4. Fry on medium-low heat until golden, then cool fully before storing.

Use your family’s customary recipe and food-safety practices; proportions differ by household.
